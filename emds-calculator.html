Coefficients come from the revised dataset (800 vendor listings; Nutsche filters n = 58 after screening). Installed power is vendor nameplate power; electricity is E = LF·(P/Vm)·Vb·τ/η for each unit, summed over the process. Unit intervals are 95% prediction intervals (×/÷ GSD² at Level 1, ×/÷ 102·RMSE at Levels 2–3); the process interval is from Monte Carlo with independent errors between units. Pooled filter and dryer models are not offered because subtypes differ too much. At Level 1 the class value of P/Vm is used directly (E = LF·(P/Vm)·Vb·τ/η), so installed power and operating hours, which need Vm, are not reported for those units. Thermal duties are not included; add the loads listed under “Not included” for each unit.
