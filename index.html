Welcome. I'm Daqian Jiang.

Assistant Professor at the University of Alabama

We work at the intersection of environmental engineering, sustainability science, and data-driven problem-solving. Our goal is to tackle challenges in water quality, chemical safety, and industrial decarbonization — and to build a lab culture that is supportive, ambitious, and balanced.
